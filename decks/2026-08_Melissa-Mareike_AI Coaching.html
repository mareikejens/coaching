§02 · How This All Works

Context, in layers.

Claude is only ever as good as what it knows about you. That knowledge lives in plain text — .md files are just text with structure.

Each layer loads on top of the last: who you are, then the project, then the recipe, then today's ask.

For our work together, one file matters most →

the taskToday's actual ask — the only part you type fresh
skills/Repeatable recipes — “do it my way, every time”
THIS ONE, FOR USproject.mdOne per project: goal, status, decisions, next steps. The enablement project's brain — every session starts by reading it, ends by updating it
CLAUDE.mdWho you are, how you work, house rules — always loaded
